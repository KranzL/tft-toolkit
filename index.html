TFT Toolkit

Two tools for Teamfight Tactics Set 18, Enchanted Wilds. Everything runs in your browser from Riot's game data and public match statistics. No account, no backend.

Emblem Planner

Pick the emblems you are holding. Get the boards that turn them into the most trait breakpoints, who should hold each emblem, and a level-by-level path to get there.

Meta Watch

What Master+ players are climbing with right now: comps, rising and falling trends, region-by-region splits including China, units, traits and emblems. Add your own Riot key to sample ladder players directly.
